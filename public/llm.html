About Rustic Retreat Weddings

Rustic Retreat Weddings is a private 65-acre outdoor wedding venue located just 50 minutes north of Edmonton, Alberta, near Lac La Nonne. We specialize in multi-day wedding celebrations where couples and their guests can enjoy an immersive experience surrounded by nature.

Our philosophy is "Ceremony Fancy, Party Casual" - we provide the space and freedom for couples to create their dream celebration without the restrictions of traditional venues.

Family-owned since: 2006 | Opened to weddings: 2025

Wedding Styles We Host

The same two packages cover every style below - these are not separate products or price tiers, just different ways couples use the property.

Camping Weddings

A true camping wedding weekend: overnight camping is included for up to 60 guests in tents and RVs (boondocking-style, no defined sites; 8 RVs included, maximum 15 total), with nightly campfires on 65 private acres. One wedding per weekend, so the property is never shared. Guests who would rather not camp can stay at Airbnb rentals 5-15 minutes away, or have an RV delivered through RVezy.
Camping wedding details

DIY Weddings

A DIY wedding venue with no mandatory vendors, no per-plate catering minimums, and no corkage fees - bring any caterer and run your own bar (an AGLC Special Event Licence is required). The curated décor collection is included at no extra cost, and the multi-day booking means setup takes days rather than hours. All-in pricing from $6,500.
DIY wedding details

Elopements and Small Weddings

There is no minimum guest count. An elopement or small wedding gets the same exclusive access to all 65 acres, the newlywed cabin, and camping for the people who come along - about an hour from Edmonton, with no crowds and no other event sharing the property.

Policies

Quiet Hours

  • Wedding Night: Midnight
  • All Other Nights: 11:00 PM to 8:00 AM

Alcohol

BYOB (Bring Your Own Beverage) is permitted and you run your own bar, with no corkage fees. The host is required to obtain an AGLC (Alberta Gaming, Liquor and Cannabis) Special Event Licence.

Vendors

Complete vendor freedom, with no mandatory vendors and no per-plate catering minimums. Bring your own caterers, photographers, florists, DJs, and any other vendors.

Tours

Property tours are available by appointment only and are scheduled around existing bookings to protect client privacy.

Wedding Season

June through September

Booking Policy

Minimum 3-day gap between bookings to ensure the property is prepared specifically for each couple. This limits availability to 12-15 weddings per season.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the wedding season?

June through September.

How many guests can attend?

Up to 80 guests for ceremony and reception. Up to 60 guests can camp on-site.

Is alcohol allowed?

Yes. BYOB is permitted and you run your own bar with no corkage fees. The host needs an AGLC Special Event Licence.

What are the quiet hours?

Midnight on the wedding night. 11pm to 8am on all other nights.

Is there electricity?

Yes, solar power and running water are available. There is a wash house with two shower stalls on on-demand hot water, and a bridal suite with two hair and makeup stations.

How far from Edmonton?

99 km northwest, approximately one hour drive, near Lac La Nonne.

Can we bring our own vendors?

Yes, complete vendor freedom is included - no mandatory vendors and no per-plate catering minimums.

What do the packages cost?

2027 pricing: 3-Day Weekend: $6,500. 5-Day Celebration: $7,500 (recommended).

Can guests stay overnight at the venue?

Yes. Overnight camping is included with every package for up to 60 guests (tents and RVs; 8 RVs included, maximum 15 total). The newlywed cabin sleeps 4.

Is the venue dog-friendly?

Yes, well-behaved pets are welcome. A small pet cleaning fee applies for dogs staying in the cabin.

Where do non-camping guests stay?

Airbnb rentals are available 5-15 minutes away. RVezy trailer delivery is also an option.
