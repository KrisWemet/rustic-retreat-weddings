Rustic Retreat Weddings

The Venue

65 private acres of forest and meadow, a cabin for the two of you, and a shed full of décor you can use for free.

Venue Highlights

  • Clear-top gazebo for receptions or ceremonies (seats 80)
  • Ceremony spots in the open meadow, the poplar grove, forest clearings or the gazebo
  • Newlywed cabin that sleeps 4, a few steps from the fire pit
  • Overnight camping included for up to 60 guests, in tents or RVs
  • Wash house with two shower stalls and on-demand hot water
  • Bridal suite with two hair and makeup stations
  • Décor collection included at no extra cost
  • Yard games, a playhouse, a trampoline and a treehouse

The Property

All 65 acres are yours for the whole booking, with room for the ceremony, the reception and everything in between. Most couples pick their ceremony spot by walking the land during their visit, then move the party to the clear-top gazebo in the evening.

After a night by the fire, there's a hot shower waiting in the wash house, and the bridal suite gives the wedding party a proper place to get ready. The outhouses are kept clean, we bring in an extra portable one for bigger groups, and a bathroom house with two flush toilets is in the works for the 2027 season.

Mosquitoes vary a lot from year to year, and a few Thermacell repellers (bring your own) keep the gazebo comfortable.
