Rustic Retreat Weddings

Wedding Packages & Pricing

Multi-day wedding weekends with exclusive access to 65 acres, a newlywed cabin, camping space, and the décor collection.

Included in Every Package

  • Exclusive use of the whole 65-acre property, with one wedding per weekend
  • Set up for weddings of 60 to 80 guests, with ceremony and reception seating for 80
  • Newlywed cabin that sleeps 4
  • Overnight camping for up to 60 guests (tents and RVs; 8 RVs included, 15 maximum)
  • Wash house with two hot-water shower stalls, and a bridal suite with two hair and makeup stations
  • Clear-top gazebo, dance floor, sound system, décor collection and a full firewood shed

2027 and 2028 Wedding Packages

The Classic 3-Day Weekend: $6,500 for 2027, $7,500 for 2028

Friday to Sunday, with check-in from 8am on Friday and checkout at 8pm on Sunday.

The Full 5-Day Experience: $7,500 for 2027, $8,500 for 2028 (most popular)

Wednesday to Sunday or Thursday to Monday. The extra days are when families get to know each other, and they make setup easier if friends and family are decorating.

Prices in CAD, GST not included. Payment plans are available, and a deposit holds your date. If your wedding is less than 90 days away, the full price is due when you book.
