Vows Under the Trees

Rustic Retreat is a family-owned forest wedding venue on 65 private acres near Lac La Nonne, in Lac Ste. Anne County, Alberta. Couples come for a woodland ceremony and stay for the weekend: no ballroom ceiling, no rigid timeline, and nobody else's wedding on the property. Ceremonies happen wherever you choose. Receptions move to the clear-top gazebo under string lights, then carry on around the fire.

Forest Wedding Questions

Can we get married in the forest at Rustic Retreat?

Yes. Couples choose their ceremony spot by walking the land: a quiet poplar grove with filtered light, an intimate forest clearing, the open meadow, or the hand-crafted gazebo set in the trees. Ceremony arches and rustic benches for up to 80 guests are included.

Where is Rustic Retreat, and how far is it from Edmonton?

3121 Township Road 572A in Lac Ste. Anne County, near Lac La Nonne: 99 km northwest of Edmonton, about an hour's drive, and about 20 minutes from both Barrhead and Onoway.

How many guests can a forest wedding here hold?

Up to 80 guests for the ceremony and reception. Overnight camping is included for up to 60 guests in tents and RVs.

What happens if it rains?

The clear-top gazebo covers all 80 guests for the reception, and the ceremony can move under it too. There is room on the property for a rented tent as well.

Do you host more than one wedding per weekend?

No. One wedding per weekend, June through September, so the property is yours alone.
