About Rustic Retreat

Family-owned since 2006, Rustic Retreat grew from annual gatherings into a multi-day wedding venue built for real connection.

At a Glance

  • Family-owned since 2006; opened to weddings in 2025
  • 65 private acres near Lac La Nonne, Alberta (about an hour northwest of Edmonton)
  • Multi-day wedding weekends, with one wedding per weekend
  • Cabin for 4, camping for 60, ceremony and reception seating for 80

Our Story

In 2006, Wilf and Patsy Ouimet bought 65 acres near Lac La Nonne to build an off-grid homestead. A small gathering in the first year became an annual tradition, and some years brought 80 people camping across the property.

When Wilf finished the first part of the gazebo in 2018, he told Shannon, "Looks nice. Good place to get married." Chris's brother married here that year, and Chris and Shannon married here in 2019. They moved to the property full-time, worked through dozens of smaller projects after COVID pushed up wood prices, and opened to weddings in 2025.

The family hosts every weekend themselves: Shannon handles communication and scheduling, Chris looks after the land and logistics, Wilf builds the bigger projects, and Patsy keeps the cabin and grounds in shape.
