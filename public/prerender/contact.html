Tours

Tours are by appointment on weekdays from May to September, scheduled around existing bookings, and run up to 90 minutes. You'll walk the ceremony and reception spaces, see the cabin and camping areas, browse the décor collection and talk through packages and pricing, with no pressure to book.

If you live too far away to visit, a video call tour counts as your visit. Shannon replies within 24 hours to set up your tour.
