PACKAGES
---------

--- THE FULL 5-DAY EXPERIENCE (Most Popular / Recommended) ---
Duration: Thursday to Monday (108 hours)
Price: $7,500 CAD (2027 season); $8,500 CAD (2028 season)

Timeline:
- Thursday: Check-in opens at 8am, and by afternoon there are tents and trailers between the poplars. That evening, both families sit down to the first dinner together while someone gets the fire going.
- Friday: Friends and family help set up the ceremony spot and dress the gazebo with pieces from the décor collection. Then comes the rehearsal, and a dinner that runs long because nobody has anywhere to be.
- Saturday: Get ready in the bridal suite, then walk out to the meadow at golden hour to say your vows. After that, the party moves under the string lights in the gazebo and can go all night. One couple kept it going until 5am.
- Sunday: There's no rush to pack, so the day is yours to fill. Couples here have planned slip-and-slides, inflatable obstacle courses and all-out Nerf wars. That night, everyone gathers around the fire one last time.
- Monday: Linger over breakfast, then pack up between hugs. Checkout isn't until 8pm, so the goodbyes can take as long as they need to.

Best for: Couples who want their wedding to be a whole weekend that feels like them, from the first dinner to the last goodbye. The activity day is where that shows the most, and it's been one of the highlights for couples who've done it.


--- THE CLASSIC 3-DAY WEEKEND ---
Duration: Friday through Sunday (60 hours)
Price: $6,500 CAD (2027 season); $7,500 CAD (2028 season)

Timeline:
- Friday: From 8am, guests roll in, set up camp and wander the trails while the gazebo is decorated for tomorrow. By dark, the first campfire is lit and everyone's catching up.
- Saturday: Start with a slow coffee, then head to the bridal suite for hair and makeup. You say your vows in the evening light, and the dance floor stays busy as late as you want it to.
- Sunday: Sleep in, then swap stories from last night over a late breakfast. Nobody has to rush the takedown or the goodbyes, since checkout isn't until 8pm.

Best for: Couples who want a classic Friday to Sunday wedding weekend at a lower price, especially when vendors are handling setup and takedown. Most guests only need to take the Friday off, and there are still two nights around the fire.
