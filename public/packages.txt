PACKAGES
---------

--- THE FULL 5-DAY EXPERIENCE (Most Popular / Recommended) ---
Duration: Wednesday to Sunday or Thursday to Monday (108 hours)
Price: $7,500 CAD (2027 season); $8,500 CAD (2028 season)

Timeline (Wednesday arrival option):
- Wednesday: Guests arrive through the day, set up camp and sit down to a first dinner together.
- Thursday: A slower day to settle in, with a hike, some yard games and a fire in the evening.
- Friday: Setup, the rehearsal and a rehearsal dinner.
- Saturday: Your wedding, with a golden-hour ceremony and the reception after dark.
- Sunday: A slow breakfast and long goodbyes, with checkout at 8pm.

Best for: Couples who want their families to get to know each other, and anyone who has left a six-hour wedding feeling they barely talked to the people they came to see.


--- THE CLASSIC 3-DAY WEEKEND ---
Duration: Friday through Sunday (60 hours)
Price: $6,500 CAD (2027 season); $7,500 CAD (2028 season)

Timeline:
- Friday: Check-in from 8am. Guests arrive through the day and set up camp.
- Friday evening: Time to explore the trails, then the first campfire.
- Saturday: Your wedding day, from getting ready in the bridal suite to dancing in the gazebo.
- Sunday: A slow morning and goodbyes, with checkout at 8pm.

Best for: Couples on a tighter schedule or budget, or with guests travelling from far away who need set arrival and departure days.

FOR GUESTS WHO DON'T CAMP
--------------------------
Half a dozen Airbnb properties sit within 5 to 15 minutes around the lake. RVezy.com delivers trailers right to the camping area and sets them up. Comfortable for elderly guests or families with infants.


FLEXIBLE ARRIVAL & DEPARTURE
------------------------------
- Arrival: Property is yours from the start of booking. No strict check-in time.
- Departure: You have the ENTIRE last day. No 11am hotel checkout.
